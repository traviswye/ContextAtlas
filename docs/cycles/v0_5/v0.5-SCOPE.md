## v0.5 thesis

**Close v1.0 ship-gate criterion #1 (efficiency + quality wins
under full quality-axis methodology) by shipping the rigorous-
evidence foundation for ContextAtlas's claims: full statistical
bounded-validity (n≥5 + CIs); blind-graded quality measurement
with judge-agreement statistics; calls-bucket reporting; adaptive
priors. After v0.5, ContextAtlas's empirical claims withstand
scrutiny.**

Methodology-arc-first deliberately. v0.4 proved bounded validity
at n=2 with ~4-13% replication-noise-floor; the launch-document
credibility line at §8.7 of `phase-8-trace-analysis-supplement.md`
is the floor, not the ceiling. v0.5 escalates from "v0.3 findings
replicate within trial variance" to "v0.3 findings are CI-bounded
+ blind-grader-confirmed; methodology is fully defensible under
peer review."

Onboarding-arc absorbs in v0.6 because:

1. **v1.0 ship-gate criterion #2 (developer onboarding pipeline)
   is independent of criterion #1 timing.** Both must ship before
   v1.0; sequencing affects timing not whether-ships.

2. **F2 sequencing rationale (per ROADMAP §v0.5+ planning analysis):
   methodology infrastructure is heavier (~3-4 new components) than
   onboarding (~2-3 new components); doing the heavier-infra arc
   first means v0.6 has lighter setup overhead.**

3. **Real reuse opportunities exist v0.5 → v0.6 (per §9):** LLM-
   judge harness grades v0.6 init-pipeline output; statistical
   tooling extends to init-timing analysis; v0.5 methodology
   evidence informs v0.6 onboarding-design priors.

4. **Travis self-use of v0.4-shipped tool starts NOW** (pre-v0.5
   scope-doc; carries through cycle). Self-use mitigates F2's
   "rubric without real-user signals" weakness — Travis-machine
   informal use generates rubric-design feedback before Step 1
   investigation phase.

The underlying question narrows from v0.4's "does the sharpened
tool survive real-user variance?" to v0.5's "are the claims
defensible under scrutiny?"

## User-facing goal

The thesis above is the v0.5 *engineering* statement. The product
*goal* lives in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md) "What ContextAtlas Is
FOR" subsection unchanged from v0.4 ship — production-tool for
developers using Claude Code on real user repos. v0.5 specifically
delivers the **evidence package** that backs the production-tool
claim: when a developer (or a reviewer evaluating the tool)
asks "how do you know it's better?", v0.5 answers with CI-bounded
efficiency wins + blind-graded quality measurements + judge-

single-cell observations.

and product utility (per §10): rigor confirms the claims;
self-use confirms the claims hold up in real work.

When tension surfaces during Stream A/B/C execution, ROADMAP
production-tool framing wins; this document amends to follow.

## Guiding principles (inherited + new)

All v0.1/v0.2/v0.3/v0.4 guiding principles apply unchanged. The
ones that most constrain v0.5 decisions:

- **Evidence-gated upgrades.** Every v0.5 deliverable cites a
  v0.4 finding, Step 9 §8 supplement observation, or scope-
  deferred v0.4 candidate inventory item that triggered it. No
  speculative scope.
- **Methodology-as-thesis, not methodology-as-overhead.** v0.5
  inverts v0.4's "research-shaped work absorbed for launch-
  document credibility" framing. v0.5 IS the research-shaped
  work; production-tool framing returns in v0.6.
- **F2 sequencing discipline.** Onboarding-arc work that surfaces
  during v0.5 execution gets explicit "v0.6 thesis" stamps, not
  inline absorption. Mixed-thesis (F3) was explicitly rejected
  for narrative coherence; mid-cycle drift toward F3 is a
  rescope condition.
- **Bounded statistical rigor.** v0.5 ships full statistical
  bounded-validity (n≥5 + CI) on the Step 9 anchor cells +
  optional pilot expansion to tie/trick-bucket cells. NOT full-
  matrix replication (36 cells × n=5 = ~180 trials; out of
  envelope; deferred to v0.5+ stretch goal post-v1.0). Cell
  selection bounded; trial count bounded; cost envelope bounded
  per v0.4 precedent.
- **Single-judge with known limitation.** Cross-vendor judge
  panel (Claude + GPT + Gemini) would isolate per-vendor
  quality differences from rubric-fit signal but is out of
  v0.5 budget. v0.5 ships single-judge (Claude Sonnet 4.6) with
  explicit known-limitation framing per §6; cross-vendor panel
  defers to v1.x post-launch hardening.
- **Calibration-before-production discipline.** Step 6 (pre-
  flight grading calibration; small-N + two metrics) MUST clear
  pre-defined thresholds before Step 7 production replication
  starts. Mirrors v0.3 Step 9 docstring-calibration discipline
  (small-sample validation before big-N spend). Not optional;
  rescope condition if calibration fails.
- **Travis-intuition correlation as anti-runaway-judge gate.**
  v0.5 introduces a novel measurement type (LLM-as-judge);
  mitigation against "judge measures wrong thing reliably"
  failure mode is Travis manually grades a calibration sample;
  judge-Travis correlation must clear threshold. Single-point
  human-anchor; not full external-graders panel (deferred).
- **Dogfood evidence as one grading substrate signal.** Self-
  use atlas already exists (v0.4 Step 11 refresh at SHA
  `8e20aae`); Step 6 calibration may use self-use trial outputs
  as one calibration data point alongside Step 9 anchor cells.
  Not load-bearing; complementary.
- **Adaptive priors update post-cycle, not mid-cycle.** Per R1
  refinement: mid-cycle priors update introduces methodology
  variance (Step 7.1 priors ≠ Step 7.5 priors). v0.5 cycle
  uses static v0.4-Step-3 priors throughout; post-v0.5
  aggregation produces adaptive priors for v0.6+ first runs.
- **One-way migrations.** Atlas schema v1.3 stays. Symbol ID
  format remains ADR-01 stable. No backward-compat cruft.

## Scope

**Conventions.** Throughout this document, *cell* = prompt ×
condition combination per v0.4 Phase 8 precedent. "5 anchor
prompts × 2 conditions = 10 anchor cells" expands to 50 trials
at n=5.

v0.5 delivers three streams. Stream A (infrastructure) is the
foundational stream; Stream B (substantive API spend) executes on
the infrastructure; Stream C (methodology riders) ships smaller
companion items. Streams have natural sequencing — see Sequencing
section.

### Stream A — LLM-judge harness + rubric protocol + double-blind harness

**Headline:** Build the methodology infrastructure ContextAtlas
needs for defensible quality-axis claims. Three core components
+ statistical tooling foundation.

**Core components (thesis-driving):**

- **LLM-judge harness.** Judge-call abstraction (Claude Sonnet
  4.6 default; Opus 4.7 escalation backup); output capture; per-
  call cost tracking; deterministic-where-possible (temperature
  set; seed if API supports); judge-agreement statistics
  computation primitives. Implementation depth: ~200-300 LOC
  across `src/grading/judge-client.ts` + `src/grading/agreement-
  stats.ts` (estimated; final placement during Step 1 design).

- **Graded-output protocol.** Rubric prompt + per-axis scoring
  + harness wiring. Rubric design lock at Step 1 (4 axes lean
  per Open Question Q2: factual correctness; completeness;
  actionability; hallucination; 0-3 scale). Rubric prompt
  authoring follows v0.3 Step 9 docstring-calibration prompt-
  authoring discipline (prompt + 2-3 worked examples + edge-
  case handling). Implementation depth: ~150-250 LOC + rubric
  prompt text (TBD authoring at Step 1; estimated 2000-4000
  characters per `EXTRACTION_PROMPT` precedent).

- **Double-blind harness.** Output anonymization (strip
  condition labels ca/beta-ca/alpha/beta from output text +
  metadata; strip filename markers like `compact_format.txt`;
  randomize A/B presentation order with logged seed); judge-
  agreement metrics (within-judge consistency via re-grade
  same trial; cross-presentation-order agreement; Travis-
  intuition correlation captured at Step 6). Implementation
  depth: ~150-250 LOC across anonymization pipeline + agreement
  metrics + post-hoc verification (>60/40 position correlation
  triggers style-normalization stretch goal per §6).

**Statistical tooling foundation (companion to core):**

- **CI computation library.** 95% CI on per-cell efficiency
  metrics (tokens / calls / cost) at n≥5; aggregation tooling
  for cross-cell summary. Library choice TBD at Step 1 (lean:
  Node-native simple-statistics or implement t-distribution
  CI computation directly given small dependency surface;
  decide based on Node-ecosystem licensing + dependency
  minimization principle). Implementation depth: ~100-200 LOC.

- **Per-cell + aggregate reporting infrastructure.** Phase-9
  reference doc scaffolding; auto-generated variance tables;
  per-cell + aggregate metric reporting; ship-narrative
  credibility-line generation. Implementation depth: ~100-150
  LOC across reporting scripts + doc-template.

**Stream A total estimated LOC:** ~700-1150 (core + statistical
tooling). **API spend:** ~$0-4 (probe runs only; calibration
test calls during Step 1-5 development).

### Stream B — Statistical replication + grading runs

**Headline:** Execute the methodology infrastructure at production
scale to produce v0.5 evidence package. Two big API-spend phases
+ pre-flight calibration gate.

**Pre-flight grading calibration (Step 6; gate-condition):**

- **Within-judge consistency check.** Same trial graded twice;
  per-axis score stability; aggregate consistency stat. Threshold:
  ≥X% per-axis agreement (X locked at Step 1 design phase based
  on rubric-axis design; lean ≥80% based on rubric-fit
  expectations; finalized via Step 6 calibration on Step 9
  trials).
- **Travis-intuition correlation.** Travis manually grades same
  calibration trials (3-5 trials × 4 axes = 12-20 manual grades);
  judge-vs-Travis per-axis correlation computed. Threshold: ≥X
  correlation coefficient (X locked at Step 1; lean ≥0.6 based
  on calibration-quality precedent in similar LLM-judge
  literature; finalized post-Step-1).
- **Both metrics MUST clear thresholds before Step 7 production
  replication starts.** Within-judge consistency alone could
  produce "reliable judge measuring wrong thing"; Travis-
  intuition correlation alone could produce "agrees once not
  stable." Both gate Step 7.
- **Calibration cost estimate (R2 honest):** $10-25.
  - Sonnet calibration baseline: 10 trials × 2 (within-judge
    consistency 2nd pass) × 4 axes ÷ batched-prompt-strategy
    = ~$10 floor at Sonnet pricing.
  - Opus escalation backup: +$15-20 if Sonnet calibration
    surfaces lenience/strictness vs Travis-intuition baseline
    requiring escalation.
  - Travis manual grading: $0 API; Travis-time cost only.

**Full statistical replication (Step 7; production-scale):**

- **Cell selection (Q4 lock):** 5 anchor prompts × n=5 trials ×
  2 conditions (ca + beta-ca) = 10 anchor cells × n=5 = ~50
  production trials. Per-cell n=5 floor; n=7-8 stretch on
  high-variance cells per P3 refinement (Step 6 calibration
  surfacing >20% per-cell variance on existing Step 9 anchors
  triggers stretch).
  - **Anchor cells:** httpx/p4-stream-lifecycle (Theme 1.2
    fix anchor; ca + beta-ca); cobra/c3-hook-lifecycle (win-
    bucket; beta-ca); httpx/p2-http3-transport (win-bucket;
    beta-ca); hono/h1-context-runtime (win-bucket beta-ca;
    Step 9 outlier 45% token Δ — stretch n=7-8 likely
    triggered); cobra/c4-subcommand-resolution (Theme 1.1
    multi-symbol API closure; ca + beta-ca).
- **Optional Step 7 stretch goal:** 2-3 tie/trick-bucket cells
  × n=5 to characterize the *non-win* prediction. Cost adder:
  ~$10-15. Decide at Step 6 calibration completion based on
  remaining budget.
- **Trial methodology:** identical to Step 9 (no prompt
  changes; no condition changes; only re-runs); v0.4-shipped
  atlases as substrate (apples-to-apples vs Step 9 trials).
- **Cost estimate:** ~$25-40 base ($0.50-0.75 avg per trial
  × 50 trials); +$10-15 if stretch expansion triggered.

**Grading run (Step 8; blind-grade Step 7 outputs):**

- **Scope:** All Step 7 production-replication outputs blind-
  graded across 4 rubric axes; per-axis judge-agreement
  statistics captured; per-cell + aggregate scoring.
- **Cost estimate:** ~$15-25 ($0.30-0.50 avg per grading-call
  × 50+ grading calls; depends on judge cost-per-call ×
  output count × axes).
- **Output anonymization protocol enforced** per Stream A
  double-blind harness; post-hoc position-correlation
  verification per §6.

**Stream B total API spend:** $50-90 base; $50-105 with stretch
(Step 7 tie/trick expansion + Step 6 Opus escalation both
triggered). Fits within v0.5 three-tier framing per §Cost
envelope; both-stretches-trigger projection ($112 with atlas
refresh + infra-dev) crosses the $100 absolute upper bound and
requires Travis rescope decision per §Cost envelope.

## Sequencing

Streams have natural sequencing — Stream A is foundational
infrastructure; Stream B executes on it via calibration → production
replication → grading; Stream C riders ship after Stream B
completes (process improvements + adaptive priors aggregation).

**Stream A → Stream B → Stream C rationale:**

- **Stream A first because methodology infrastructure must exist
  before it can be exercised.** LLM-judge harness, graded-output
  protocol, double-blind harness, statistical tooling — all need
  to be implemented + tested before Step 6 calibration can run.
  Stream B running on un-built infrastructure is impossible.
- **Stream B middle because the substantial API spend (Steps 6-
  8) requires Stream A complete + calibration-gate-passed.**
  Step 6 calibration is the gate-condition; Steps 7-8 production
  replication + grading depend on calibration thresholds clearing.
- **Stream C last because methodology riders (#9 calls-bucket,
  #12 adaptive priors) absorb v0.5 evidence findings (Step 7
  trial data feeds adaptive priors aggregation; Step 8 grading
  outcomes inform call-bucket reporting calibration).** Process
  improvements (#7 schema-driven tests, #8 CLAUDE.md formalization)
  can ship anytime but cluster naturally with Stream C cycle-
  close work.

**Within Stream A:**

- Step 1 design phase before any implementation (rubric design;
  judge-model selection; anonymization protocol; statistical
  methodology lock).
- Step 2 LLM-judge harness before Step 3 graded-output protocol
  (rubric needs judge-call abstraction to wire against).
- Step 3 protocol before Step 4 double-blind harness (anonymization
  wraps the protocol).
- Step 5 statistical tooling parallel-able with Step 4 (independent
  code surface; can land any order post-Step-3).

**Within Stream B:**

- Step 6 calibration MUST land before Step 7 production. Hard
  gate per guiding principle.
- Step 7 production replication before Step 8 grading. Grading
  runs on Step 7 outputs; sequence is mechanical.

**Within Stream C:**

- #9 + #12 absorb after Step 8 grading completes (both consume
  Step 7-8 trial/cost data).
- #7 + #8 process improvements can land any time during cycle;
  cluster with Stream C for single coherent ship.

**Reasonable execution order:**

1. Step 1 investigation + design (Stream A).
2. Steps 2-5 infrastructure implementation (Stream A).
3. Step 6 pre-flight calibration (Stream B; gate-condition).
4. Step 7 production replication (Stream B; biggest API spend).
5. Step 8 grading run (Stream B; second-biggest API spend).
6. Step 9 synthesis (Stream B closure; phase-9 reference doc;
   named v0.5 findings).
7. Step 10 methodology riders (Stream C; #9 + #12 + #7 + #8).
8. Step 11 v0.5 ship gate (matches v0.4 Step 11 single-ship-
   commit pattern).

Step ordering details land in `STEP-PLAN-V0.5.md` (next session).
Expect 11 numbered steps based on this decomposition (matches
v0.4's 11-step structure).

## Cost envelope

**Cost envelope: $51-97 base projection; $80 rescope-
investigation trigger; $100 absolute upper bound triggers
Travis rescope decision.**

Three-tier framing differs from v0.4's single-tier (ceiling =
upper bound) precedent. v0.5 introduces this distinction
because methodology-novelty cost variance is genuinely higher
than v0.4's hardening work — judge calibration novel; rubric-
fit uncertain; grading per-call cost depends on output length
distribution. Three-tier framing accommodates legitimate
uncertainty without forcing compression into single-ceiling
discipline.

$80 trigger preserves v0.4 cost-disclaimer investigation
pattern (treat envelope-approach as signal to investigate
rather than raise the cap). $100 absolute upper bound applies
if Step 6 calibration escalates (Sonnet → Opus) AND Step 7
stretch expansion (tie/trick-bucket cells) both trigger; in
that case Travis rescope decision required.

v0.5 splits API spend across infrastructure-development cost
(small) + Stream B production cost (substantial):

**Infrastructure-development budget (~$0-4):**

Steps 1-5 implementation. Probe runs during harness development;
calibration test calls during rubric protocol authoring. Trace-
level spend; absorbed via dev-time ad-hoc API calls.

**Stream B production budget: $50-90 base; $50-105 with stretch
(Step 7 tie/trick expansion + Step 6 Opus escalation both
triggered):**

## Methodology limits acknowledged

v0.5 ships full statistical bounded-validity + blind-graded
quality measurement, but methodology limits remain. Honest framing
preserves v0.4 explicit-acknowledgment discipline:

- **Single-judge limitation.** v0.5 ships single-judge (Claude
  Sonnet 4.6) per cost-budget logic. Same training corpus + RLHF
  lineage as Opus 4.7 (matrix-run model); cousins not strangers.
  Cross-vendor judge panel ({Sonnet, GPT-4o, Gemini-2.x}) would
  isolate per-vendor quality differences from rubric-fit signal
  but is out of v0.5 budget. **Cross-vendor judge panel deferred
  to v1.x post-launch hardening to isolate per-vendor quality
  differences from rubric-fit signal.** Doesn't block v0.5 ship;
  explicit about residual confound.

- **Within-judge stochasticity.** v0.5 mitigates via Step 6
  consistency check (same trial graded twice; per-axis score
  stability). Threshold-gated; Step 7 production blocks if
  consistency below threshold. Residual stochasticity remains
  within threshold band; reported in phase-9 reference doc.

- **Output style leakage.** v0.5 anonymization protocol
  (5 steps per Q3 lean) reduces but cannot eliminate style-
  based label leakage (compact-vs-verbose output formats may
  leak condition labels even with metadata stripping).
  Mitigation: judge prompt explicit instruction "ignore output
  format, evaluate substance against rubric." Post-hoc
  verification: position correlation > 60/40 triggers style-
  normalization stretch goal (re-render outputs through uniform
  formatter; re-grade). Deferred unless triggered.

- **n=5 vs full statistical rigor.** Per-cell n=5 is CI-
  computation floor (below = embarrassingly small-N for 95%
  CI bounds). Full-matrix replication (36 cells × n=5) is
  ~5x v0.5 cost; deferred. v0.5 ships finding-anchored cells
  (10 cells × n≥5; 5 anchor prompts × 2 conditions; n=7-8
  stretch on high-variance cells per P3); explicit "v0.5
  honestly-narrowed scope vs full-matrix replication v0.5+
  stretch goal" framing in phase-9 reference doc.

- **Cell selection finding-anchored, not random.** v0.5 cell
  selection inherits Step 9 anchor cells (5 finding-anchored
  cells with optional tie/trick-bucket pilot expansion).
  Generalizes within finding-anchor set; full-matrix replication
  is v0.5+ post-v1.0.

- **Quality-axis rubric is opinion-shaped, not ground-truth.**
  4-axis rubric (factual correctness; completeness;
  actionability; hallucination) reflects Travis-as-rubric-
  designer perspective on what "quality" means for atlas-
  bundled context. Different rubric designs would produce
  different quality measurements. Mitigation: rubric is
  documented + locked in Step 1; published in phase-9 reference
  doc; subject to community-evidence revision in future
  cycles.

## Rescope conditions

v0.5 rescopes (not kills — rescopes) if:

- **Step 6 calibration thresholds fail.** Within-judge consistency
  OR Travis-intuition correlation below threshold. Recovery path:
  Step 1 rubric design re-evaluation; Opus-as-judge escalation
  ($15-20 budget impact); rubric refinement and re-calibration.
  If second calibration fails, surface to Travis rescope
  decision: descope to 3-axis rubric OR descope to no-quality-
  axis-grading + statistical-only-rigor framing for v0.5 ship
  (criterion #1 partially closes; full quality-axis defers to
  v0.5.5 or v0.6 carry-forward).

- **Step 7 statistical replication surfaces unexpected variance
  beyond Step 9 §8 baseline.** If per-cell variance >50% on
  multiple cells (vs Step 9's 1/5 cell at 45% outlier),
  bounded-validity claim weakens. Recovery path: stretch n=7-8
  on flagged cells (cost adder $5-10); document expanded
  variance in phase-9 reference doc; preserve "BOUNDED" framing
  with refinement. If variance >100%, escalate — methodology
  itself surfaces concerns; full quality-axis rubric design
  reconsideration warranted.

- **Step 8 grading surfaces honest limits.** Quality-axis
  blind-grading reveals "ties on most non-architectural prompts;
  wins on architectural-intent prompts only" (RUBRIC prediction
  from v0.1 Phase 5). Not catastrophic; ship narrative
  honestly-narrowed: "v0.5 ships statistically-defensible
  efficiency wins + quality measurements that confirm RUBRIC
  prediction; quality-axis rubric refinement v0.6+ if community
  evidence warrants."

- **Cost envelope ceiling pressure.** If actual spend approaches
  $80 ceiling before Step 8 grading completes, treat as signal
  to investigate per v0.4 Q5 cost-disclaimer pattern. Possible
  causes: Step 6 calibration escalation to Opus-judge; Step 7
  stretch expansion triggered; Step 8 per-grading-call cost
  higher than estimated. Recovery path: pause Step 8 selectively;
  preserve Step 7 statistical replication completeness; ship
  v0.5 with statistical-only-rigor + grading-deferred framing
  if unrecoverable.

- **Mixed-thesis (F3) drift.** If onboarding-arc work surfaces
  during Stream A/B execution and absorption pressure mounts,
  resist via F2 sequencing discipline. Onboarding-arc work
  gets explicit v0.6 thesis stamps, not inline absorption.
  v0.5 thesis coherence preserved; F3 drift was explicitly
  rejected for narrative reasons.

- **External-facing target evidence bar.** If during v0.5
  execution the launch-document drafting reveals a public claim
  that v0.5 evidence can't support rigorously, that claim
  doesn't ship — even if it would have been acceptable for an
  internal milestone. Per v0.3-SCOPE.md inherited guideline
  applied to v0.5: over-shipping accuracy is preferable to
  under-shipping credibility. Higher stakes in v0.5 because
  v0.5 ship narrative IS the rigor claim.
