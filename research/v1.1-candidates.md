## Substrate-quality investigations

### 1. Symbol-attribution over-fragmentation (parseArgs concentration)

Surfaced at v0.8 close via contextatlas-on-itself dogfood: 72
claims attached to `parseArgs` from only 4 ADRs, with 100%
attachment rates on ADRs 08 + 15 + 12 (two of which — ADR-08
config paths, ADR-15 multi-symbol MCP semantics — substantively
don't relate to CLI argument parsing).

**Hypothesis:** extraction-time `symbol_candidates` resolution
silently falls back to a popular sentinel symbol when claims don't
surface named code symbols.

**Scope:**

- Read extraction pipeline Stage 3 (LLM extraction) + Stage 4
  (symbol resolution) per DESIGN.md
- Determine whether fallback is in LLM-extraction stage (Opus 4.7
  returns inappropriate candidates) or symbol-resolution stage
  (LSP layer over-resolves to `parseArgs`)
- Consider attribution constraints: claim must mention symbol in
  claim text (not just excerpt); or reject fallback-to-sentinel
  attachments outright with warning
- Re-extract substrate atlases after attribution rule refinement
- Measure claim-distribution shift

**Composes with item 2** (both substrate-quality investigations at
the extraction-attribution layer).

### 2. cobra/c3 atlas-induced over-exploration

Option B surfaced higher tool-call count at `beta-ca` vs `beta` on
the hook-lifecycle question (`beta`: 1/4/6 calls; `beta-ca`:
13/9/11 calls — 2–3× increase). Hypothesis: atlas's hook-lifecycle
claim set may have many cross-references (PreRun →
PersistentPreRun → RunE → PostRun symbols) that triggered
branching exploration. **The single atlas-induced-inefficiency
case in the Option B dataset — qualitatively distinct from the
priors-driven `beta=1.000` cells, which were 0-tool-call
pretraining answers.**

**Scope:**

- Inspect cobra atlas's hook-lifecycle claim cluster +
  cross-reference density
- Trace `beta-ca` trial logs to confirm cross-reference fan-out
  mechanism
- Consider attribution constraints: limit cross-reference
  recursion at retrieval-time, or compact bundle shape that
  consolidates cross-references

Composes with item 1 (both substrate-quality investigations at
extraction-attribution layer).

### 3. BM25 quality-axis measurement

v0.8 Ship 4a empirically validated BM25 activation at the
ordering-shift layer (divergence@5 measurements at hono v0.8-cli
show every densely-attached symbol reorders top-5 under BM25=on).
Whether BM25-reordered bundles produce *better* Claude responses
(answer quality, factual correctness, actionability) is untested
at v0.8.

**Scope:**

- Extend Ship 4a methodology with LLM-judged answer-quality
  grading
- Comparison: BM25-on vs BM25-off bundles, fixed atlas substrate
- Per-bundle rubric scoring (factual correctness, hallucination,
  actionability, completeness)
- Closes the dormant-capability-activation-validates-mechanically-
  but-quality-evidence-deferred gap (cycle-observation 22)

## Measurement-substrate refinements

### 4. Full v0.5-rigor paired-t at v0.8 substrate

Option B ran n=3 trials per (cell, condition). v0.5 ran n=27
baseline. Statistical power at n=3 is marginal for distinguishing
"atlas-aided ≈ vanilla" from "atlas-aided > vanilla" at the
bucket-prediction threshold. Single-trial outliers dominate the
mean at variance-bound cells (cobra/c5 alpha variance: 0.125 with
mean 0.500 → CV ~70%).

**Scope:**

- Run n=27 paired-t at v0.8 substrate scale on the 8 intersection
  cells
- Full v0.5 rubric application (no metric drift this time)
- Comparison: alpha-vs-ca (cleanest atlas-effect control) +
  beta-vs-beta-ca (cohort UX deployment surface)

### 5. Bucket-design re-calibration at v0.8+ substrate

Option B bucket predictions (calibrated at v0.5 with implicit
answer-quality model) didn't transfer cleanly to v0.8 tool-call-
count metric (3/8 prediction pass-rate). Cycle-observation 20:
when substrate evolves OR metric character shifts, bucket-design
intent must be re-derived.

**Scope:**

- Re-design bucket intent for current metric (tool-call
  efficiency) + current substrate (10-12 ADRs + docstring +
  commit streams)
- Pre-register predictions against the new bucket design before
  measurement
- Possible expansion to 24-cell pool (held-out p7-p12 cells from
  v0.5 cycle reserved for Step 13)

### 6. Quality-axis matrix methodology

Cycle-observation 18: empirical-design-hypothesis-falsification at
first-real-measurement surface. Tool-call count alone is a thin
axis. v1.1 measurement matrix candidate: tool-call count + answer
quality (LLM-judged) + cost + wall-clock per trial, providing
multi-axis substrate evidence rather than single-axis efficiency
framing.

### 7. Cell expansion (24-cell from 18-cell)

Held-out p7-p12 cells from v0.5 cycle reserved for "Step 13"
expansion. Worth incorporating at v1.1 measurement runs alongside
re-calibrated bucket design.

## Capability refinements

### 8. Soft-chain-α future alternative (BM25 ranking variant)

ADR-16 Decision §2 documented chain α (BM25 dominates; severity is
tiebreaker, not primary sort) as the v0.3 ship choice. Ship 4a
empirical evidence confirms: BM25 promotes context-severity claims
that mention the symbol by name above hard-severity claims that
don't.

Soft-chain-α future alternative (flagged in v0.3 Step 7):
severity-first BM25 ranking variant for cohort users who prefer
"show me hard invariants first" reading pattern over name-mention-
first relevance.

**Scope:**

- Implement soft-chain-α as config-flag alternative to chain α
- ADR-16 amendment documenting both chains
- Doctor-script recommendation: which chain at which atlas density
- Cohort-feedback-driven post-launch (defer until cohort surfaces
  severity-first preference at scale)

### 9. Target-repo SKILL.md substrate-currency drift discipline

Cycle-observation 17: SKILL.md files at user-installation-site can
drift behind canonical substrate. v0.8 surfaced this at the
Skill-substrate v0.7.1 ship; closed reactively via v0.7.2 hotfix.

**Scope:**

- `contextatlas refresh-skills` CLI subcommand for explicit
  user-side refresh
- Or: doctor check for SKILL.md substrate-version drift with
  warning
- User-facing message pattern for stale-skill detection

**Scope:**
