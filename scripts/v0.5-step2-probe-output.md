## Findings

### Finding 1 (resolved): Criterion 3 floor over-cautious

Original floor (50) was set without empirical anchoring on what a
compliant compact JSON response looks like in tokens. Resolved at
d4e9d4d with floor lowered to 20.

### Finding 2 (Step 6 calibration substrate): hallucination=1 on verifiable answer

Sonnet scored hallucination=1 on this answer despite the answer
containing real, verifiable references:

- `httpx/_models.py:635-639` — actual file:line in httpx source
- ADR-05 quotes — directly from the locked httpx ADR set
- `BoundSyncStream` / `BoundAsyncStream` — actual httpx symbols

Three plausible interpretations:

1. **Sonnet weak on httpx internals.** May not have strong training
   knowledge of httpx-specific symbols; treats specific file:line
   references as "unverifiable" without ground-truth context →
   classifies as fabrication.
2. **Placeholder rubric anchors underspecified.** The Step 2.4 probe
   used a placeholder rubric (Step 3 will commit canonical); Axis 4
   anchors may not fully convey the "verifiable references"
   distinction.
3. **Sonnet caught a subtle overclaim.** The answer claims
   "BoundSyncStream/BoundAsyncStream record response.elapsed on
   close" — possibly a slight overclaim Travis didn't notice.

**Step 6 calibration with Travis-intuition baseline will adjudicate
which holds. Probe alone cannot distinguish — this is exactly the
kind of signal the calibration step is designed to disambiguate.**

This finding is **calibration substrate for Step 6**, not a probe
failure. If the same pattern recurs at Step 6 calibration with
factual-axis correlation < 0.6 AND other axes pass thresholds, the
Step 1.3 Option A→B pivot triggers (inline ADR ground-truth context
for Step 8 grading per ADR-19 §3 Option B). The probe generated
empirical evidence that this risk is genuine, not hypothetical.

### Finding 3 (Step 6 calibration substrate): bitwise-identical scores across runs at temperature 0

Two probe runs on identical input produced bitwise-identical scores
(factual=2, completeness=3, actionability=3, hallucination=1),
identical token counts (1310 input / 35 output), identical cost
($0.004455). Wall-clock varied ~10% (1296ms vs 1446ms; network
noise) but Sonnet's outputs are perfectly stable.

This is meaningful Step 6 calibration substrate: at temperature 0,
Sonnet 4.6 may be MORE deterministic on small-output workloads than
the "approximately-deterministic" caveat in ADR-19 §2 +
judge-client.ts module header suggested. The within-judge
consistency check at Step 6 (≥80% within-1-point per axis floor)
may exceed threshold substantially.

**Finding 3 holds for compact-JSON output workloads (35 tokens;
strict format constraints from placeholder rubric). May not
generalize to verbose grading-rationale workloads if Step 3
canonical rubric design produces longer outputs. Step 6 calibration
on canonical rubric is the empirical test; v0.5 design preserves
the "approximately-deterministic" caveat as conservative default.**

n=2 substrate; not load-bearing, but a directional signal worth
carrying into Step 6 expectations.
